# Case Study -NovaMart E-Commerce Platform
## Organisation / Scenario
NovaMart E-commerce Platform
## Nature of the system
NovaMart is a simulated e-commerce platform that allows customers to browse products, place orders, make payments, review products and track deliveries
## Database Users
- Customers
- Administrators
- Warehouse staff
- Customer support staff
- Delivery staff
## Major Data Stored
- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Suppliers
- Orders
- Order items
- Payments
- Inventory
- Deliveries
- Reviews
## Key Database Operations
- Register customers
- Add and update products
- Search products
- Place orders
- Process payments
- Update stock
- Track deliveries
- Submit reviews
- Generate sales reports
